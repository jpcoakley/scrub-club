WANT IN?

Scrub Club is a beer-league team. We are not good, we are not trying to be, and we would rather have skaters who show up than skaters who can dangle.

The league
8 teams, it's chill. No contact. Need to be 40+ years old.
When
Tuesday/Thursday ice, usually late. Most puck drops land between 9:00 and 10:30 PM.
Commitment
Pay the season fee, come when you can. Everyone rotates through beer duty.
Subs
Always welcome, skaters and goalies both. Subbing is the usual way onto the roster.
How to get on the list

Talk to any Scrub Club player about joining or subbing, or email the team. Check the roster to see who you already know.
